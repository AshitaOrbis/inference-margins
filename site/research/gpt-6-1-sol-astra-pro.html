Our reading

GPT-6.1 Sol is OpenAI's successor to GPT-6 Sol, released on 2026-09-29 at the same $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input halved to $0.10 (5% of the input price, where GPT-6 Sol charges $0.20). The model was two days old when the run was made, so almost nothing about how it is served is public, and the run started from the GPT-6 Sol estimate of 2026-09-28 and said, input by input, what it carried and what it changed.
