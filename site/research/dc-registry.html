Coverage accounting

The three named-site / programme-type / generic-fill parts partition the modeled blend and sum to 100. The SKU/workload count-backed share is non-additive: it says how much of the same blend rests on a disclosed count for the exact key at the relevant workload and date, and it is never added to the three. Percentages are DERIVED from the stored key-level evidence and the selected modeled blend; the registry stores none of them.
